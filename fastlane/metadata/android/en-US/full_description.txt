Puesto Idle is a cozy idle cooking tycoon set in a Latin street food stand. Fry churros, bake empanadas, fold tacos and turn your little corner stand into the most famous spot in the barrio.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Tap each station to cook and sell. Every batch earns money.
• Buy more fryers, ovens and griddles: every level is worth more, and at levels 10, 25, 50 and 100 they cook twice as fast.
• Hire Rosa, Beto, Lupe and Mateo: they cook for you, and your stand keeps selling even when the game is closed.
• When your stand is famous, move to a new barrio: you start over, but every reputation star adds 10% to all sales, forever.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
• Never an ad when you open the game.
• Reward videos are optional: watch one only if you want to double your earnings.
• Play offline.
• No forced waiting: you progress by cooking, not by watching a timer.
• One optional purchase removes ads. No subscriptions, no passes.

FLAVORS AND BARRIOS
Cinnamon-sugar churros, hand-crimped empanadas, tacos on fresh tortillas and arepas off the budare. Start at La Esquina and move up to La Plaza, El Mercado, La Feria, La Costanera and El Centro.

If you like cooking games, restaurant games or relaxing idle tycoon games, open your stand and start selling.
